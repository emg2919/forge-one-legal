Privacy Policy

Effective August 26, 2026 · Forge One App (Forge)

Who we are

Forge One App (“Forge,” “we,” “us”) is a gym companion published by Landjack, LLC. This policy explains what the iOS app does with your information. Bundle identifier: com.forgeone.app.

What the app stores

Forge keeps the data you enter so the app can work:

  • Profile details such as date of birth, height, weight, goal, and lift baselines
  • Workouts, sets, weights, custom workouts, and history
  • Meal and calorie logs
  • Settings such as units, rest timer, and meal reminders

This data stays on your device. If iCloud sync is on, it is also stored in your personal iCloud account via CloudKit. We do not operate a Forge account system and we do not run our own servers for this data.

Apple Health

If you allow it, Forge writes completed workouts and estimated active calories to Apple Health. Forge does not read Health data. You can revoke access in the iOS Health or Settings app. Health information is governed by Apple’s Health privacy rules in addition to this policy.

iCloud

Optional iCloud sync uses Apple CloudKit so your profile, workouts, and meal logs can appear on your other devices signed into the same Apple ID. Apple provides that storage. We cannot see your iCloud contents.

Notifications

Optional local meal reminders are scheduled on your device. They are not sent through our servers.

What we do not do

  • No advertising
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs
  • No sale or sharing of personal data with data brokers
  • No third-party login

Children

Forge is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your choices

  • Turn off Apple Health or iCloud in iOS Settings or in Forge Settings
  • Turn off meal reminders in Forge Settings
  • Delete the app to remove on-device data. iCloud copies remain until you delete them from your Apple ID / iCloud storage

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
