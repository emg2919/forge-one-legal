Forge One App (Forge) · com.forgeone.app

Get help

Forge is a gym companion for logging lifts, custom workouts, meals, and history. There is no login and no Forge account.

For help, open an issue at github.com/emg2919/forge-one-legal/issues or use the contact link on the App Store listing. Include your iOS version and what you were doing when something went wrong.

Apple Health

Forge can save a finished workout and estimated active calories to Apple Health. It does not read Health data.

  • Allow access when Forge asks, or later in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Forge
  • If nothing appears in Health, complete a workout in Forge, then check the Health Workout category

iCloud sync

When sync is enabled, your profile, workouts, and meal logs use your personal iCloud account.

  • Sign in to iCloud on the device and turn on iCloud for Forge
  • Settings in Forge should show iCloud as Enabled
  • The first sync can take a few minutes

Workouts and meals

  • Today starts bench, squat, or a workout you create
  • In Settings, swipe left on an exercise or a round to delete it
  • Custom workouts are saved on the device and do not change the bench → squat rotation
  • Meal reminders are optional and scheduled on your device
